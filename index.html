Working Papers

Priority Design for Grant-Funding Policies

Revise & Resubmit, Games and Economic Behavior

Abstract

Public research funders such as the NIH allocate grants that differ in size, can be partially funded, and may count toward reserves for every category they belong to. I study the design of priority-based funding rules in this setting, for two classes of problems: earmark problems and floor problems. For earmark problems I characterize a serial rule that respects merit and category reserves; for floor problems I propose a floors-first rule and establish an impossibility for type-monotonic rules. I also analyze the computational complexity of both rules.
